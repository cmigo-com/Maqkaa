## Pricing approach
Prices are Maqka Summit starting-price suggestions informed by public Kenya market references. They are not copied competitor prices and must be reviewed by the operator before publishing as final quotations.

## Research used
- SafariBookings for broad Kenya safari package/duration/price comparisons.
- Danil Scenic Tours for published Kenya safari package structures and inclusions.
- Baecation for Kenyan hiking destination and day-hike price benchmarks.
- Touch Wild Tours for a published Mount Longonot group-hike price and inclusions.
- Kenya Wildlife Service for current Mount Longonot visitor fees and visitor guidance.
- Wikimedia Commons for selected reference gallery images with source/licence notes.

## Important image note
Some newly added destination cards use external reference images from Wikimedia Commons or representative editorial imagery. Replace these with Maqka Summit-owned/licensed destination photography before commercial publication wherever possible.
